# Feed loading and city identities

The first feed load shows five skeleton stat cards. Each skeleton has the same
label, action header, and five 64-pixel rows as a loaded stat preview.

City ranking entries use an internal identity in the form
`city:country:city-name`. The feed hides this internal identity and shows the
city name from the ranking entry instead. The city row shows the competition
under the city name and the person under the result. The country is shown only
by the flag.
